Welcome To Fazbear Frights

A place where frights and fears come to life!

Fazbear Frights is a small local horror attraction based on the tragedies that occured in the fazbear diner and pizzarias. We want to offer some light to the events that occured in a horrific and entertaining fashion.

Real Authentic Props!

We have spent the time to go through the now shutdown locations to collect props, spare parts, and animatronic pieces! With collecting as much as we can from these locations we hope to make an environment accurate to the real places!
